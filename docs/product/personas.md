# Personas

## Sunita — Class 9 learner (primary)

- Shares a low-end Android phone; Odia-medium; little English.
- ~0.8 MB of mobile data to spare, ~25 minutes in the evening.
- Wants to actually *understand*, not just finish homework.
- Demo profile: `sunita` → focus concept `electric-current`.

## Asha — Class 9 learner

- Slightly more data/time (1.2 MB, 30 min), comfortable switching to English.
- Has a partial, wrong idea (force as a "stored" quantity).
- Demo profile: `asha` → focus concept `force-as-interaction`.

## Teacher (Sasmita)

- 40–60 learners, one Monday-morning prep window.
- Wants to know **which misconception is common** and **what to do about it**,
  not a dashboard of individual scores.
- Consumes the Teacher Action Brief; never sees learner free text by default.

## Field coordinator

- Sets up phones, seeds packs, checks sync health.
- Cares about data cost and whether the app works with the network off.
